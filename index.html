At a glance

Samuel Lefcourt is a PhD candidate at Johns Hopkins University, advised by Dr. Craig Jones and Dr. Gregory Falco as a member of the Radiology AI Lab and Institute of Assured Autonomy. His research focuses on building trusted AI systems for safety-critical applications where failure results in dire repercussions. This includes radiomic diagnostics and treatment planning within healthcare and characterizing electronic warfare attacks on satellite systems within space defense. His work has been presented to a wide audience, spanning radiology venues to international conflict-adjacent zones.
